Our Story

Oh लतिका

Oh Latika isn't named after a girl.

It's named after a feeling.

The feeling of being remembered. The feeling of being chosen. The feeling of finding beauty in ordinary days.

Who is a Latika?

A little romantic.
A little nostalgic.

She finds beauty in little things.

She chooses softness, again and again.

She reaches for cotton, block prints, and pieces that feel like home.

She doesn't need to be the loudest in the room to be remembered.

She is a Latika.

Maybe she's someone you know. Maybe she's you.

The Craft

Before it becomes a garment, it begins as a craft.

Carved by hand

Wooden blocks, chiselled motif by motif in Jaipur, each one a small, imperfect original.

Printed, not pasted

Every block is dipped, placed and pressed by hand — no two lengths of fabric ever fall the same.

Woven in cotton

Breathable, soft-washed cotton, chosen for how it lives on you — not just how it looks in a photo.

Carved wooden blocks. Skilled hands. Cotton. Ink. Time. Generations of tradition — made in India.
